# Music Cleaner

**Developer:** [0xEtherPunk](https://github.com/0xEtherPunk) | **GD:** 0xPSP

Batch delete downloaded custom songs to free disk space while keeping your favorites protected.

## How to Use

1. **Star Favorites:** Click the star on any song row or level page to protect it from deletion.
2. **Open Browser:** Click the music button next to folders in the Saved Levels page.
3. **Delete All Unstarred:** Click the red cross button in the bottom-right corner to delete all unstarred songs at once.
4. **Delete Single Song:** Click the trash icon on a song row to delete only that specific track.
5. **Safety:** Starred songs cannot be deleted individually or in batch until unstarred.
