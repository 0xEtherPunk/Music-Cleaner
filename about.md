# Music Cleaner

**Developer:** [0xEtherPunk](https://github.com/0xEtherPunk) | **GD:** 0xPSP

Utility for managing downloaded custom songs and batch cache cleanup in Geometry Dash.

## Usage

1. **Star Favorites:** Click the star icon on any song row or level page to protect it from deletion.
2. **Open Browser:** Click the music button next to the folder button in the Saved Levels page.
3. **Batch Purge:** Click the trash icon in the song browser header to delete all unstarred tracks. Favorited songs remain intact.
4. **Protection:** Favorited songs cannot be deleted individually or in bulk until unstarred.
